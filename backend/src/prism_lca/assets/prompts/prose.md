Write the interpretation chapter of an ISO 14044 life cycle assessment report.
Work from the data and the study metadata handed to you — **and from what you look up.** This chapter answers *why*, and the material for a why is one level below what you were given: `explain_result` (`mode="tree"`) says what sits inside the process that dominates, `mode="flows"` says which emission carries it, and `get_process_details` says what a background dataset actually is. An interpretation written without opening anything can only rearrange the results table.
- Name the dominant contributors and say what drives them. Go past the aggregated phases: the hotspot list names the individual processes — the leading ones with their reference product, their own dataset description, their classification and the inputs the calculation attributes impact to — and the inventory names the elementary flows carrying the score. Use them to explain *why* a process dominates, in your own words: a description is material to paraphrase, never to quote at length. Say which dataset and which emission is responsible, with placeholders ({{hotspots.0.name}}, {{hotspots.0.share_text}}, {{inventory.flows.0.flow}}). A placeholder whose name ends in _text already carries its percent sign or unit — do not add one.
- **Compare with published studies — only from research findings.** If research_web is offered and the facts name no published LCA of a comparable product, search for one before you write this part. Where the facts carry research findings (`web_research`) or your own research_web lookups name published LCAs of comparable products, say where this result stands against them: name each study and its source in words, quote its figure as the finding states it, and say what differs — functional unit, system boundaries, method, data year — before you compare. A gap those differences explain is the finding; a match is not a validation. Without such findings, say nothing about other studies.
- State plainly where a result rests on a single assumption or a proxy dataset; the `exchange_notes` say why an input was chosen.
- Draw the conclusion the numbers support, and no stronger one.
Do not restate the tables. Do not praise the study. No closing summary.
